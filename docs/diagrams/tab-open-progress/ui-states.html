The open ends one of three ways. On success the clicked tab becomes active and the history skeleton takes over. On failure the spinner and overlay go away and the error bar or the disconnected screen (with Reconnect) shows, as before. On a cancel (the overlay's button, or a click on the tab still on screen) the open's late result is ignored.
